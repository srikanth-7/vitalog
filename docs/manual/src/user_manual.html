1. Welcome

Vitalog keeps every lab report for everyone you look after — you, your partner, your children, your parents — in one place. Take a photo of a report or pick a PDF, and Vitalog reads it for you: every test, its value, the lab's normal range, and whether it's high or low. It sorts each test into groups like Blood, Thyroid, Kidney and Vitamins, and shows how each value changes from report to report.

Your reports stay on your phone. Vitalog reads them right here on the phone. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you sign in and tap Sync — and even then your data is locked so that only your family's phones can read it.

What Vitalog does not do

  • It does not diagnose or give medical advice. It shows what your lab printed. Always talk to your doctor about your results.
  • It does not give you a "health score".
  • It never asks you to type results in. If it isn't sure about something, it marks it Needs review so you can check it against the paper.

Words used in this manual

3. Finding your way around

Home before anything is added

Everything you read is at the top of the screen; everything you do is in the tray at the bottom, where your thumb reaches.

The bottom tray

  • Person button (left) — shows whose reports you are looking at. Tap it to switch to another family member or add one.
  • Add a report — the main button.
  • Sync — appears on the Reports tab when you're signed in.
  • Tabs — the selected tab shows its name; the others show only their picture:

4. Add your family

Add family member
  1. Go to the Family tab and tap Add family member (or tap the person button in the tray, then Add family member).
  2. Type their name and choose how they're related to you (You, Partner, Parent, Child…).
  3. Enter their age and choose their sex (or leave it as Not set). Vitalog uses these only to pick the right range when a lab didn't print one — it never guesses them.
  4. Tap Add member.
  5. To add a photo, press and hold the member later → Edit details.

Switch person

Tap the person button in the tray and pick someone. You stay on the same tab. Tapping someone on the Family tab takes you to their Home.

Edit or remove

On the Family tab, press and hold a member → Edit details or Remove. If they have reports, Vitalog tells you how many will be removed before you confirm.

5. Add a lab report

Three ways to add a report
  1. Make sure the right person is shown in the tray.
  2. Tap Add a report.
  3. Choose:
    • Scan with camera — lay the report flat in good light; the scanner finds the page edges.
    • Choose from Photos — a photo or screenshot of the report.
    • Choose a file — PDF, Word, Excel, CSV, text, web page or an email with the report attached.
  4. The report appears on the timeline as Reading…. You can keep using the app.
  5. When it's done, a message says how many tests were added and whether any need attention. Tap View to open it.

The first time, Vitalog asks whether to lock your original documents with Face ID / fingerprint. See chapter 12.

Vitalog also checks for you

  • Same report twice — if you add a report that's already there (the same file, or another photo of the same paper), Vitalog keeps one copy and tells you.
  • Wrong person — if the name printed on the report doesn't match the person you chose, the timeline asks: Keep here or Move to… another member.

7. Categories

Tested groups

The Categories tab groups every test by body system — Blood & CBC, Glucose, Kidney, Liver, Lipid, Thyroid, Vitamins, Iron, Urine and more.

  • Tested groups come first. If a group's usual tests haven't been done yet, it says Not yet: … with their names.
  • Yet to test groups follow, greyed out.
  • Some groups (pancreas, cancer markers, hormones, infections, clotting) are tests done when your doctor advises; they are never shown as missing.

Tap a group to see one tile per test, with its latest value and how many readings there are.

8. A test's history and fixing a value

A test's history

Tap any result to see its whole history: a chart of every reading over the normal-range band, and a list of every dated value with its lab.

If something was read wrong

  1. Open the test and tap Edit value at the bottom.
  2. Type what's printed on your report — value, unit or range. Vitalog checks it against the range again.
  3. Use Compare with original to see the report next to the value.
  4. If the test is already correct, tap Looks right.
  5. For a test Vitalog didn't recognise, pick its group. Vitalog remembers it for future reports.

When nothing is left to check, the report changes from Needs review to Ready.

10. Reminders

Reminders

Set a reminder for the next blood test, a thyroid recheck, or adding the latest report.

  1. Tap the bell on Home (or Family → Reminders).
  2. Tap Add reminder, give it a title, choose the person, the date and how often it repeats.
  3. Allow notifications when asked. Vitalog explains why first; if you said no, it offers Open Settings.

The notification shows only your title and "Reminder for {name}" — never test names or values, so nothing private shows on the lock screen.

12. Privacy and security

  • Read on your phone — reports are read on the phone, never by an online service.
  • Locked on your phone — results, family details, photos and reminders are stored encrypted. The key never leaves this phone.
  • Original documents are encrypted separately. You can ask for Face ID / fingerprint / phone PIN each time an original is opened: Vitalog asks before your first report, and you can change it in Settings. It locks again after 2 minutes away from the app.
  • App switcher — when you switch apps, Vitalog's preview is covered so results don't show.
  • Permissions — before the camera, photos, Face ID or notifications prompt, Vitalog explains in plain words why it needs it.
  • Sync — your data is sealed on the phone before it's sent. The server can't read names, tests, values or dates. Original documents are never uploaded.

13. Account, Sync and backup

Account card on the Family tab

Sign in

Family tab → Account → Sign in. Use Google, or email and password (you'll get a confirmation email; Forgot password sends a reset link).

Sync (back up)

  1. Go to the Reports tab.
  2. Tap Sync in the tray.
  3. The first time, Vitalog shows your recovery key. Tap Save to my passwords (or Copy and keep it somewhere safe — the copy clears from the clipboard after a minute).
Keep your recovery key. It's the only way to open your backup on a new phone if your phone's own keychain backup isn't there. Vitalog can't reset it for you. You can see it again on the Family tab.

New phone

Install Vitalog and sign in. Your family and reports come back by themselves; if asked, enter your recovery key.

Sign out / delete account

Family tab → account card. Delete account removes your account and everything backed up; what's on the phone stays until you remove it.

Sync, restore and sharing need a build connected to the Vitalog server. In builds without it, these buttons say "Cloud sync isn't set up in this build yet."

14. Share with family

Share a family member's reports with someone else — for example, so your sibling can also see your mother's reports. Everyone with access is equal: they can add reports and see new ones.

Invite someone

  1. Family tab → press and hold the member → Share.
  2. Tap Invite someone. Vitalog backs up that member and opens your phone's share sheet with an invite link.
  3. Send the link by WhatsApp, SMS or email. Each link works once and expires.

Accept an invite

  1. Tap the link (or Family → Open an invite link and paste it).
  2. Vitalog shows who shared whom and how many reports. Choose who this person is to you.
  3. Tap Add to my family. Their reports appear on Home.
  4. If the invite is for you and you already have a "You" profile, Vitalog offers to Combine them.

See or stop sharing

Press and hold the member → Share shows who has access and pending invites. Remove someone, or cancel an invite. Removing the member from your own phone means you leave; the others keep their reports.

The secret part of an invite link never reaches the server, so only the person you send it to can open the reports.

15. Big screens and holding the phone sideways

On a phone that opens up (like the iPhone Duo or an Android foldable) or a tablet held sideways, Vitalog uses the extra space:

  • The tray stands on its side along the left edge — person at the top, tabs in the middle, Add a report at the bottom.
  • Screens split in two: Home shows the status on the left and history on the right; Categories shows groups and results; Reports shows the timeline and the open report; Family shows people and settings.
  • Folded or held upright, Vitalog looks just like it does on a normal phone.

Questions

Is my data sent to the internet? Only when you tap Sync, and then only in sealed form the server can't read. Your original documents never leave the phone.

Can Vitalog tell me if I'm ill? No. It shows what your lab printed. Please talk to your doctor.

What if the lab didn't print a range? Vitalog may use a standard "usual range" — only if it knows the unit, the person's sex and age — and labels it as such. Otherwise it marks the test Needs review.

Do I need an account? No. Everything except backup and sharing works without one.
